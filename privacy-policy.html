Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This privacy policy explains how MedLib handles any personal information when you use our website.

Information We Collect

No personal data is collected by MedLib. We do not use cookies, analytics, or any tracking tools. The website is completely static and does not require registration or login.

Third‑Party Services

MedLib uses the following third‑party services that may collect anonymous usage data:

  • Firebase (Google) – Used only for reader counters (number of times a book is opened). This stores an anonymous count per book, not tied to any user.
  • Puter AI – The Nexus assistant uses Puter’s cloud AI; it processes your question and returns an answer, but no personal data is stored or shared.

These services are subject to their own privacy policies, which you can review on their respective websites.

Your Choices

You can block third‑party services using browser extensions or settings. MedLib will still function normally without them (counters may show 0).

Changes to This Policy

Any future changes will be posted on this page. The last update was March 2026.
