Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Welcome to MedLib. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using MedLib, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

MedLib does not require registration, login, or any personal information to access our textbooks, MCQ banks, or AI assistant. You are not asked to create an account, provide your name, email address, or any other identifying information. Therefore, we do not collect any personal data directly from you.

If you choose to contact us via email or the contact form, we will receive your email address and the content of your message. This information is used only to respond to your inquiry and is not stored for any other purpose. We do not share your contact information with third parties unless required by law.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information (Anonymous)

When you visit MedLib, certain non‑personal information may be collected automatically by our servers or third‑party services. This may include:

  • Your IP address (anonymised where possible).
  • Browser type and version.
  • Operating system.
  • Referring website (if you came from a search engine or another link).
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and click behaviour.

This information is aggregated and used solely to improve the performance, content, and user experience of MedLib. It is not linked to any individual identity.

1.3 Cookies and Similar Technologies

MedLib itself does not set any cookies. However, some third‑party services we use (such as Google AdSense and Cloudflare) may place cookies to provide their services. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block third‑party cookies entirely.

For more information about how Google uses cookies in advertising, please visit Google's Advertising Policies.

2. Third‑Party Services

MedLib integrates the following third‑party services, each of which has its own privacy policy:

2.1 Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. AdSense may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your browsing activity. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings. You can also disable cookies entirely in your browser.

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

2.2 Cloudflare (CDN and Security)

MedLib uses Cloudflare as a content delivery network (CDN) and security provider. Cloudflare may collect anonymised request data (e.g., IP addresses, browser fingerprints) to detect and block malicious traffic, optimise performance, and prevent DDoS attacks. This data is not used for advertising purposes.

Cloudflare's Privacy Policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

2.3 Puter AI (Nexus Assistant)

When you use the Nexus AI assistant, your question is sent to Puter's API (powered by Google Gemini). Puter processes the question and returns an answer. Puter does not store personal data or retain the content of your questions beyond the time needed to generate a response. For more information, see Puter's Privacy Policy.

2.4 GitHub Pages (Hosting)

MedLib is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs (IP addresses, request timestamps) to ensure the reliability and security of their platform. GitHub does not use this data for advertising.

GitHub's Privacy Policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement

3. How We Use Your Information

Because we collect minimal information, our usage is limited to:

  • Improving our website – aggregated analytics help us understand which content is most popular, where users encounter difficulties, and how to optimise performance.
  • Security – we use anonymised IP addresses and request data to detect and prevent abuse, DDoS attacks, and malicious bots.
  • Responding to inquiries – if you contact us, we use your email address only to reply.
  • Displaying relevant advertisements – Google AdSense may use cookies to show ads that are relevant to your interests (you can opt out).

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent any personal information to third parties. We may share anonymised, aggregated data (e.g., “1000 users visited the Cardiology section last week”) with partners for research or marketing purposes, but this data cannot be used to identify you.

We may disclose information if required by law (e.g., in response to a court order or subpoena) or to protect the rights, property, or safety of MedLib, our users, or others.

5. Data Retention

Logs from Cloudflare and GitHub Pages are retained for a limited period (typically 30–90 days) and then automatically deleted. Google AdSense may retain cookie data according to its own retention policies (usually up to 13 months). Any emails you send us are kept for as long as necessary to resolve your inquiry, after which they are deleted.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR for EU residents, CCPA for California residents), you may have the right to:

  • Access – request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction – request that we correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion – request that we delete your personal data.
  • Opt‑out of cookies – adjust your browser settings to block third‑party cookies.
  • Opt‑out of personalised ads – visit Google's Ad Settings.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the email address in the Contact Information section below. Because we collect very little personal data, these requests will typically be resolved quickly.

7. Children's Privacy

MedLib is intended for medical students, residents, healthcare professionals, and educators. It is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us, and we will delete it immediately.

8. International Data Transfers

MedLib is hosted on GitHub Pages (servers located in the United States) and uses Cloudflare's global CDN (which may route traffic through various countries). Google AdSense may also process data in multiple countries. By using MedLib, you consent to the transfer of anonymised data to these countries, which may have different data protection laws than your own.

9. Security Measures

We take reasonable precautions to protect the information we collect. These include:

  • Using HTTPS (SSL/TLS) to encrypt all data transmitted between your browser and our servers.
  • Employing Cloudflare's DDoS protection and web application firewall.
  • Regularly updating our software and monitoring for vulnerabilities.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Third‑Party Links

MedLib may contain links to external websites (e.g., Unsplash for images, Amazon for affiliate links). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any external site you visit.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of MedLib after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.

12. Contact Information

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us:

  • Email: medlib@example.com
  • Via our Contact page

We will respond to all legitimate requests within 30 days.

Thank you for trusting MedLib. We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing free, high‑quality medical education.
