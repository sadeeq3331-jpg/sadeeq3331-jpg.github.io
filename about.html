About MedLib – Free Medical Textbook Library

MedLib is a completely free, open‑access medical education platform created by Abubakar Sadeeq. It was born from the belief that medical education should be accessible to everyone, regardless of financial circumstances. Many students around the world struggle to afford expensive textbooks and question banks – MedLib removes that barrier.

What We Offer

MedLib provides:

  • 150+ textbooks covering basic sciences, organ systems, and clinical specialties.
  • 500+ USMLE Step 1 MCQ questions with detailed explanations, formatted in the new USMLE block style (20 questions per 30‑minute block).
  • 500+ USMLE Step 2 CK MCQ questions focusing on clinical decision‑making and management.
  • Nexus AI Assistant – an AI that answers medical questions, explains concepts, and pronounces terms in US/UK English.
  • Interactive book viewer with table of contents, search, and reading progress bar.
  • Personal study tools – favourites, bookmarks, recently viewed, and conversation export.

Content Quality

All textbooks are original compilations by Abubakar Sadeeq, based on peer‑reviewed sources, medical school curricula, and USMLE content outlines. Each chapter includes clinical correlations to help students apply theoretical knowledge to patient care.

Why Free?

The site is supported by minimal, non‑intrusive advertising (Google AdSense). This allows us to keep all educational content free forever. No registration, no hidden fees – just open access to high‑quality medical resources.

Our Mission

To democratise medical education by providing a comprehensive, free, and user‑friendly digital library for medical students and professionals worldwide.
