About MedLib – The World's Largest Free Medical Textbook Library

Welcome to MedLib, a completely free, open‑access medical education platform created by Abubakar Sadeeq. Our mission is to democratise medical knowledge by providing high‑quality textbooks, interactive question banks, and AI‑powered study tools to students, educators, and healthcare professionals worldwide – without any cost or registration.

📖 What You'll Find Inside MedLib

MedLib is designed to cover the entire medical school curriculum, from foundational sciences to clinical practice. Here's a detailed breakdown of our content:

1. Basic Sciences (50+ Textbooks)

  • Biochemistry & Molecular Biology – Protein structure, enzyme kinetics, DNA replication, transcription, translation, metabolic pathways (glycolysis, TCA cycle, oxidative phosphorylation, gluconeogenesis, glycogen metabolism, pentose phosphate pathway), lipid metabolism, amino acid metabolism, and clinical correlations (e.g., G6PD deficiency, phenylketonuria).
  • Genetics – Mendelian inheritance, chromosomal abnormalities, molecular genetics, epigenetics, population genetics, genetic counselling, and common disorders (cystic fibrosis, sickle cell anaemia, Huntington's disease).
  • Immunology – Innate and adaptive immunity, antigens, antibodies, MHC, T‑cell and B‑cell development, hypersensitivity reactions, autoimmunity, immunodeficiency (primary and secondary), vaccines, and transplant immunology.
  • Microbiology – Bacteriology, virology, mycology, parasitology, pathogenicity, antimicrobial agents, and infectious disease mechanisms.
  • General Pathology – Cell injury, inflammation, repair, haemodynamic disorders, neoplasia, immunopathology, and environmental diseases.
  • General Pharmacology – Pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics, drug receptors, drug metabolism, adverse effects, and principles of therapeutics.
  • Research Methodology & Biostatistics – Study designs, data collection, statistical tests, evidence‑based medicine, and research ethics.

2. Organ Systems (60+ Textbooks)

  • Cardiovascular System – Cardiac anatomy, electrophysiology, haemodynamics, ischaemic heart disease, heart failure, arrhythmias, valvular diseases, congenital heart defects, hypertension, and pharmacology (beta‑blockers, ACE inhibitors, calcium channel blockers, diuretics).
  • Respiratory System – Lung anatomy, ventilation, gas exchange, V/Q mismatch, obstructive lung diseases (asthma, COPD), restrictive lung diseases (interstitial fibrosis, sarcoidosis), pulmonary infections, lung cancer, and pharmacology (bronchodilators, corticosteroids).
  • Neurology – Neuroanatomy, neurophysiology, stroke, seizures, neurodegenerative diseases (Alzheimer's, Parkinson's), demyelinating disorders (multiple sclerosis), neuromuscular diseases, headache, and neuropharmacology.
  • Gastrointestinal System – GI anatomy, motility, secretion, digestion, absorption, liver function, pancreatic function, GI infections, inflammatory bowel disease, peptic ulcer disease, cirrhosis, hepatitis, and GI pharmacology.
  • Endocrinology – Hormone synthesis, regulation, feedback loops, diabetes mellitus, thyroid disorders, adrenal disorders, pituitary disorders, reproductive endocrinology, and pharmacology (insulin, oral hypoglycaemics, thyroid hormones).
  • Renal System – Kidney anatomy, glomerular filtration, tubular function, acid‑base balance, electrolyte regulation, acute kidney injury, chronic kidney disease, glomerulonephritis, and renal pharmacology (diuretics, ACE inhibitors).
  • Reproductive System – Male and female reproductive anatomy, gametogenesis, menstrual cycle, pregnancy, parturition, lactation, contraception, infertility, STIs, and reproductive pharmacology.
  • Musculoskeletal System – Bone and joint anatomy, muscle physiology, osteoporosis, arthritis (osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, gout), muscle disorders, and pharmacology (NSAIDs, DMARDs, bisphosphonates).
  • Hematology – Blood cell formation, anaemias, leukaemias, lymphomas, bleeding disorders, thrombosis, and haematological pharmacology.

3. Clinical Specialties (40+ Textbooks)

  • Dermatology – Skin anatomy, inflammatory skin diseases, infections, neoplasms, and dermatological pharmacology.
  • Psychiatry – Mood disorders, anxiety disorders, psychotic disorders, personality disorders, substance abuse, and psychopharmacology.
  • Ophthalmology & ENT – Eye and ear anatomy, common disorders (cataracts, glaucoma, otitis media, sinusitis), and treatment options.
  • Pediatrics – Growth and development, paediatric infections, congenital disorders, child nutrition, and paediatric pharmacology.
  • Emergency Medicine – Trauma, resuscitation, acute poisoning, medical emergencies, and emergency pharmacology.
  • Toxicology – Poisoning mechanisms, antidotes, overdose management, and environmental toxins.
  • Clinical Skills – Physical examination, history taking, diagnostic reasoning, and procedural skills.
  • Nutrition & Metabolism – Macronutrients, micronutrients, vitamins, minerals, nutritional disorders, and metabolic diseases.

4. USMLE Question Banks

We provide two massive MCQ banks to help you prepare for the United States Medical Licensing Examinations:

  • USMLE Step 1 – 500+ high‑yield questions covering biochemistry, genetics, immunology, microbiology, pathology, pharmacology, and systems. Each question includes detailed explanations and is formatted in the new USMLE block style (20 questions per 30‑minute block).
  • USMLE Step 2 CK – 500+ clinical vignettes focusing on diagnosis, management, and next steps. Perfect for clerkship and residency preparation.

🤖 Nexus AI Assistant – Your Personal Study Partner

Nexus is an intelligent medical chatbot integrated into every book and MCQ bank. It uses the latest generative AI (powered by Google's Gemini model) to answer your questions in real time. You can:

  • Ask any medical question – Nexus provides evidence‑based, educational answers.
  • Highlight text in any book – a popup appears with options to ask Nexus, or pronounce the text in US or UK English (with speed toggle).
  • Get pronunciation help – hear difficult terms spoken in natural American or British voices, with adjustable speed (normal or slow).
  • Save flashcards – convert any Q&A pair into a flashcard for later review.
  • Export conversations – save your chats as PDF or text files for revision.

📚 How to Use MedLib Effectively

Our platform is designed for effortless navigation:

  • Browse by subject – use the category cards on the homepage to jump directly to any discipline.
  • Search – type keywords into the search bar to find specific topics across all textbooks and MCQ banks.
  • Open a book – click any book card to launch the interactive book viewer. Use the sidebar to navigate chapters, and the built‑in search to find text inside the book.
  • Track your progress – mark books as favourites, bookmark important pages, and see your recently viewed items on the homepage.
  • Dark mode – toggle dark theme for comfortable night reading.
  • Keyboard shortcuts – press ? for help, Esc to close the book viewer, and Ctrl+K to focus the search bar.

👨‍⚕️ Who Is MedLib For?

  • Medical students – preparing for USMLE Step 1, Step 2 CK, or medical school exams.
  • Residents and fellows – quick reference for clinical practice.
  • Physicians and healthcare professionals – staying up‑to‑date with core medical knowledge.
  • Educators – free teaching resources for lectures and courses.
  • International medical graduates (IMGs) – preparing for USMLE and clinical practice in the US.

💡 Why MedLib Is Completely Free

The high cost of medical textbooks and question banks creates an unfair barrier to education. MedLib was created to remove that barrier. The platform is funded solely by minimal, non‑intrusive advertising (Google AdSense) – we do not charge users, and we never will. No subscription, no paywalls, no hidden fees.

By using MedLib, you support a sustainable model that keeps medical education accessible to everyone, everywhere.

🔧 Technical Features & Accessibility

  • Fully responsive – works seamlessly on desktops, tablets, and smartphones.
  • Offline‑friendly – once loaded, textbooks are cached for offline reading (progressive web app features coming soon).
  • Fast loading – content is served via Cloudflare CDN, with lazy‑loaded images and optimised code.
  • Accessibility – ARIA labels, keyboard navigation, and high contrast for users with visual impairments.
  • Secure – HTTPS with free SSL certificate, and secure third‑party integrations.
