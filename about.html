About MedLib

MedLib is a completely free medical textbook library created by Abubakar Sadeeq to help medical students, residents, and healthcare professionals access high‑quality educational resources without financial barriers.

All content is curated from reliable sources and organized by medical school curriculum – from basic sciences to clinical specialties. Each textbook is presented in an interactive HTML format, works on all devices, and includes built‑in search and navigation.

Additionally, MedLib offers over 1,000 USMLE‑style multiple‑choice questions for Step 1 and Step 2 CK preparation, with detailed explanations and progress tracking.

Why Free?

Medical education should be accessible to everyone. Many students struggle with the high cost of textbooks. MedLib exists to remove that obstacle, providing a complete library of resources that you can use anytime, anywhere.

What’s Inside

  • 150+ textbooks covering all major subjects
  • 500+ USMLE Step 1 practice questions with answers and explanations
  • 500+ USMLE Step 2 CK practice questions with answers and explanations
  • Interactive MCQ banks with instant feedback
  • Search, category filtering, and responsive design
  • AI assistant (Nexus) to answer medical questions

Who Is It For?

MedLib is designed for medical students, residents, fellows, and educators. Whether you're preparing for exams, refreshing your knowledge, or teaching others, you'll find valuable resources here – all free.

About the Creator

Abubakar Sadeeq is a medical educator and web developer who believes in open access education. He built MedLib to share the resources he wished he had during his own studies. The library continues to grow with new content added regularly.
